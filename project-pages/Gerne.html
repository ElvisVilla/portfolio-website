Gerne

Native iOS Mobile Application

Product Designer / Software Developer

Overview

Simplifying language learning.

Gerne is an iOS learning companion for students attending language classes. The app embeds a dictionary for easy word lookup: Any word that is searched for gets saved in your personal wordlist. The purpose of the wordlist is to show the most searched words for an easier and faster learning experience. Gerne also includes a flash card game to practice your vocabulary.

  • Designed and developed the entire product proposition, focusing on improving the language learning experience for students attending language classes.
  • Designed and developed the app's entire UI, layout, interactions and animation.
  • Implemented a flash card game, personal wordList with query, filters and sorting functionalities.
  • Enabled local storage with SQLite, and Supabase as cloud storage and user authentication.
  • Implemented API request to Wiktionary for the dictionary lookup.
  • Embedded multiple Apple frameworks, such as a translator that supports 21 languages powered by TranslationKit.
