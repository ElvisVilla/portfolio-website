Overview

Creating atmosphere, one pixel at a time.

The Kindred is my "Souls" 2D game ported from Unity to the Godot Engine, with a focus on expressive atmosphere, platforming, and systems-driven gameplay. Since porting I have further developed the game. My goal is to publish this game in the future.

  • Developed gameplay systems: player mechanics, enemy AI, combat systems, save system, and level transitions.
  • Designed and animated characters, enemies, environment art, parallax backgrounds, and tilemaps.
  • Created and implemented the HUD, inventory, and character panel.
  • Built atmosphere through lighting, post-processing effects, particle systems, and sound effects.
  • Designed levels and environment art with an emphasis on platforming mechanics.
